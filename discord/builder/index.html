Copyright check before you download

Only add images, videos, or music that you are allowed to use. This helps protect you and the community from copyright problems under Austrian law.

  • You made it: You created the image, video, or music yourself.
  • You have permission: You bought it or received permission to use it. Upload proof if you have it.
  • It is free to use: It is clearly marked Public Domain or CC0.

Important:

If you use media without permission and someone makes a copyright claim, you are responsible for that media and any related costs. If you are unsure, ask an adult or use a trusted free-media library.

Need free media with clear usage rules? Try a trusted library such as https://pixabay.com.
