Our Custom Bots

We run three of our own bots so we don't need clunky public bots.

@Role Bot Verification & Roles

Keeps out spam bots and lets you pick your own roles through simple commands[cite: 1].

/verify Quick captcha to unlock the server (1-hour cooldown if you fail)[cite: 1]
/age Pick your age group[cite: 1]
/game Pick games you play to unlock channels[cite: 1]
/gender Choose your gender identity[cite: 1]
/sexuality Choose your sexuality[cite: 1]
/pronouns Display your preferred pronouns[cite: 1]
/beta apply Apply to test new updates early[cite: 1]
/beta leave Leave the tester team at any time[cite: 1]
@Member Bot Activity & Updates

Takes care of server maintenance, cleans up completely inactive members, and tracks invite links[cite: 1].

Inactive Cleanup: If you don't send a message for 4 months, your roles are cleared and you only see the #inactive channel.

Getting Back: Just type /reactivate in that channel to get your normal access back.

Maintenance Mode: When we are working on updates, channels are locked and you can see updates in #update[cite: 1].

Invite Links: Connects personal Discord invites directly to custom web pages[cite: 1].

@Birthday Bot Birthdays

Remembers community birthdays so you don't have to search through chat history or pinned messages[cite: 1].

/birthday @user Instantly see someone's birthday in chat[cite: 1].

Voluntary: Synced from our internal community list only if you chose to share it[cite: 1].

Simple Server Rules

No giant legal wall of text—just plain common sense.

Don't Be a Jerk

Talk trash and banter all you want, but leave identity out of it. Racism, forcing religion down people's throats, homophobia, and political agenda pushing will get you banned immediately.

No Random Advertising

Put your stream links, TikToks, and projects in #advertisement. If you drop promo links in regular chats, they get deleted on the spot[cite: 1].

3 Strikes and You're Out

Break the rules and you get a strike. Once you hit 3 strikes, you are banned. Admin calls are final. If you see a message starting with an asterisk (*), it means it's unverified rumors[cite: 1].

Keep Voice Chats Chill

Yelling or screaming when you get jump-scared in a game is totally fine. Ear-raping your mic, spamming soundboards, and intentionally ruining games for other people is not.
